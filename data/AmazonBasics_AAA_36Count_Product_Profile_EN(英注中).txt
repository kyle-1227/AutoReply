1.2 WHAT THE PRODUCT IS（1.2 产品是什么）
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The AmazonBasics AAA Performance Alkaline Battery is Amazon's entry-level,
single-use alkaline cell. "AAA" is the international R03 / LR03 size - what
Chinese buyers know as the No. 7 battery - rated at 1.5V nominal, cylindrical,
about 10.5 mm in diameter and 44.5 mm tall. It is one of the two most common
household battery sizes; the other is AA (the No. 5 battery in China).
（AmazonBasics AAA 高性能碱性电池，是亚马逊自有品牌的基础款一次性碱性电池。
AAA 即国内常说的"7 号电池"（国际编号 R03 / LR03），单节标称电压 1.5V，
圆柱形，直径约 10.5mm、高约 44.5mm，是最常见的两类家用小电池之一
（另一类是 AA，即 5 号电池）。）

The 36-count box is built as an outer cardboard carton holding nine shrink-
wrapped cards of four cells each: tear off a card when you need batteries, and
the rest stay sealed. Reviewers bring this up again and again - it keeps the box
tidy and limits how much air the unused cells are exposed to.
（36 节装采用"纸盒外箱 + 内部 4 节一组热缩封装"的形式：
一个大纸盒里装着 9 板、每板 4 节，用一节拆一板，未拆封的继续密封保存。
这个设计在评价里被反复提到——既方便收纳，又能让剩余电池少接触空气。）

    sit unused, take the batteries out.
(3) Suspicion of counterfeits or old stock comes from two signals: packaging that
    looks previously opened, and cells that arrive flat. The remedy is simple -

PART 4  USE-CASE GUIDE (BASED ON REAL USE REPORTS)
第四部分  适用场景指南（基于真实使用反馈）
================================================================================

4.1 STRONGLY RECOMMENDED: LOW-DRAIN, INTERMITTENT DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These draw little current and idle for long stretches, which is exactly where
alkaline cells are comfortable - and where the positive reviews cluster:
  - TV / set-top box / air-conditioner remotes
  - Wall clocks, alarm clocks, thermometer-hygrometers
  - Children's sound-and-light toys, audio story players (not motor-driven)
  - Wireless mice and keyboards
  - Electronic door locks, doorbells, door and window sensors
  - Electronic candles and LED string lights (the type that run a few hours a day
    on a timer)
  - Flashlights used occasionally for emergencies
  - Small instruments such as glucose meters, thermometers, hearing aids
  - Everyday use in wired or wireless game controllers
4.1 强烈推荐：低耗电、间歇性使用的设备
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
这类设备电流需求小、待机时间长，正是碱性电池的舒适区，也是好评最集中的地方：
  · 电视 / 机顶盒 / 空调遥控器
  · 挂钟、闹钟、温湿度计
  · 儿童声光玩具、故事机（非马达驱动的）
  · 无线鼠标、无线键盘
  · 电子门锁、门铃、门窗传感器
  · 电子蜡烛、LED 装饰串灯（每天定时亮几小时的那种）
  · 手电筒（偶尔应急使用）
  · 血糖仪、体温计、助听器等小型仪器
  · 有线手柄/无线手柄的日常使用

4.2 USE WITH CAUTION: MEDIUM- TO HIGH-DRAIN DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
They work, but lower your expectations and be prepared to change them more often
than you would with name brands:
  - Intense, continuous gaming sessions
  - Children's motorized ride-on toys and motor-driven toys
  - Electric toothbrushes and shavers
  - Wireless microphones and recording gear

4.2 谨慎使用：中高耗电设备
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
可以用，但请降低预期，并做好"换得比别人勤"的心理准备：
  · 游戏手柄的高强度连续使用（长时间对战）
  · 儿童电动玩具车、马达类玩具
  · 电动牙刷、剃须刀
  · 无线麦克风、录音设备

4.3 NOT RECOMMENDED: SUSTAINED HIGH CURRENT OR HIGH-VALUE DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Camera flashes and professional photography gear - buyers report running flat
    after two RAW shots
  - High-output flashlights and headlamps for extended continuous use
  - Expensive equipment - not because the battery is poor, but because a single
    leak here costs far more than the money saved on the cells
  - Devices left unattended for long periods (an emergency kit, for example) -
    lithium cells are the better choice
4.3 不建议使用：持续大电流 / 高价值设备
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  · 数码相机闪光灯、专业摄影设备 —— 有买家反馈两张 RAW 就耗尽
  · 长时间连续工作的强光手电、头灯
  · 价格昂贵的设备（原因不是电池不好，而是这类设备一旦漏液，损失远大于
    省下来的那点电池钱）
  · 需要长期放置不管的设备（如备用应急包里的设备）—— 建议改用锂电池

PART 6  USAGE AND CARE(第六部分  使用与保养建议)
================================================================================

6.1 USING THEM CORRECTLY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Watch the polarity. On a AAA cell the flat end is negative and the end with
   the raised button is positive. Installed backwards, the device simply will not
   run.
2. Do not mix. Never combine new and old cells, and never mix brands or
   chemistries (alkaline with carbon-zinc or with NiMH). Mixing accelerates
   leakage and over-discharge.
3. Replace the whole set. If a device takes two or four cells, change all of them
   at once instead of swapping only the flat one.
4. Remove them for long storage. If a device will go unused for more than a
   month, take the cells out and store them separately. This is the single most
   effective way to avoid a leak ruining the device.
5. Never recharge, heat, or disassemble. Charging an alkaline cell risks leakage,
   swelling, and rupture.

6.1 正确使用
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 认准正负极：AAA 电池平的一端是负极、带凸起的一端是正极，装反设备不工作。
2. 不要混用：新旧电池不要混用，不同品牌/不同化学体系（碱性 vs 碳性 vs 镍氢）
   更不要混用——混用会加速漏液和过放。
3. 整组更换：设备用 2 节/4 节就一次全换，别只换掉没电的那一节。
4. 长期不用就取出：超过一个月不用的设备，把电池取出来单独存放，
   这是避免漏液毁设备最有效的一招。
5. 不要充电、不要加热、不要拆解：碱性电池充电有漏液甚至爆裂风险。

6.2 STORAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Keep them in the original box and on the original card; do not open the shrink
  wrap early.
- Store in a cool, dry place - a drawer or cupboard - away from balconies, stoves,
  and other hot, humid spots.
- Do not let them rattle around with keys, coins, or other metal objects; shorting
  is a real hazard.
- Rotate stock first-in, first-out: new boxes at the back, older ones at the
  front.
- On delivery, check the batch code and expiry date. Return anything that looks
  tampered with or feels suspiciously light.

6.2 储存
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
· 原盒原板存放，不要提前拆开热缩膜；
· 放在阴凉干燥处（抽屉、储物柜），避免阳台、厨房灶台附近的高温高湿；
· 不要和钥匙、硬币等金属物混放，防止短路；
· 按"先进先出"使用，把新买的放里面、旧的放外面；
· 收到货先翻看批号与有效期，异常批次（拆封痕迹、明显轻飘）及时退换。


6.3 CLEANING UP AFTER A LEAK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If you find white crystals or a sticky residue in the battery compartment:
1. Put on gloves. Neutralize with a cotton swab dipped in a little white vinegar
   (or lemon juice), then wipe clean with a dry swab.
2. Do not touch the residue with bare hands at any point, and wash your hands
   afterwards.
3. Let the compartment dry completely before fitting new cells. If corrosion is
   severe, have the device serviced.
4. Do not throw leaking cells in the household trash. Dispose of them with
   hazardous or battery waste according to local rules.

6.3 漏液后的处理
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
若发现电池仓有白色结晶或黏稠液体：
1. 戴手套，用棉签蘸少量白醋（或柠檬汁）中和，再用干棉签擦净；
2. 全程不要用手直接接触，处理后洗手；
3. 清理干净、彻底干燥后再装新电池；腐蚀严重的设备建议送修。
4. 漏液的电池不要随手丢进垃圾桶，按当地有害垃圾分类投放。

Q1: Is this really a AAA (No. 7) battery?
A: Yes. AAA = international R03/LR03 = the No. 7 battery in China, 1.5V. The
   No. 5 battery is AA, and this is not it. Check the marking inside the battery
   compartment before buying.
Q1：这个真的是 7 号电池吗？
A：是。AAA = 国际 R03/LR03 = 国内 7 号电池，1.5V。5 号电池对应的是 AA，
    这款不是 5 号，买之前务必确认设备电池仓上的标识。

Q2: Can I use these in a camera or flash?
A: Not advisable. Several reviews report capacity collapsing fast in high-drain
   gear (flat after two RAW shots, for instance). Use a name-brand alkaline cell
   there, or step up to lithium or NiMH.
Q2：能用在相机、闪光灯上吗？
A：不建议。评价中有多例反馈在高耗电设备上容量衰减极快（如两张 RAW 就耗尽）。
    这类设备建议用大牌碱性电池或直接上锂电池/镍氢电池。


Q3: Why is the batch I bought so short-lived?
A: The data points to batch variation. Over three years the average rating slid
   from 4.54 to 4.36, and 19% of negative reviews explicitly cite a "bad batch."
   If what arrived is clearly abnormal, ask for a replacement rather than
   concluding the product itself is bad.
Q3：为什么我买的这批特别不耐用？
A：从数据看，这更像是批次波动——三年间评分从 4.54 缓降到 4.36，差评中
    19% 明确指向"bad batch"。如果到手就明显异常，优先走退换，
    而不是直接判定产品不行。


Q4: Can they be recharged?
A: Absolutely not. This is a single-use alkaline cell. Putting it in a charger
   risks leakage, swelling, and rupture.
Q4：能充电吗？
A：绝对不能。这是碱性一次性电池，放入充电器有漏液、鼓包、爆裂风险。


Q5: Will I get through 36 cells before they expire?
A: Not at normal household usage rates. Shelf life is long (buyers report roughly
   9-10 years), and the sealed four-cell cards keep the unused ones dry. Just use
   them in expiry-date order.
Q5：36 节会不会用不完过期？
A：正常家庭用量下不会。产品保质期较长（有买家反馈约 9—10 年），
    加上 4 节一板的密封包装，剩余电池不易受潮。按有效期排序使用即可。


Q6: Do they leak?
A: 7.8% of negative reviews mention leakage or corrosion, a shared risk across all
   alkaline batteries. Reduce the odds by not mixing cells, not over-discharging
   them, and removing them from devices you are not using. If a leak happens, see
   section 6.3.
Q6：会漏液吗？
A：7.8% 的差评提到漏液/腐蚀，属于碱性电池的共性风险。降低概率的办法就是
    不混用、不过放、长期不用就取出。一旦漏液，按第 6.3 节处理。

Q7: How far behind Duracell are they really?
A: In low-drain devices (remotes, clocks, door locks, toys) users consistently say
   they cannot tell the difference. In high-drain devices (electric toothbrushes,
   wireless microphones) the users who report a clear gap put this product at
   roughly one-quarter to one-half the life of a name brand. The price gap is
   usually wider than that.
Q7：和 Duracell 比到底差多少？
A：低耗电设备（遥控器、钟表、门锁、玩具）上，用户普遍反馈"感觉不到差别"；
    高耗电设备（电动牙刷、无线麦克风）上，有明显差距的用户反馈是
    本品寿命约为大牌的 1/4 到 1/2。价格差距通常在这个差距之上。

Q8: Are they suitable for children's toys?
A: Very much so - toys are the most frequently cited positive use case (275
   mentions). Cheap, plentiful, and no heartbreak when one dies. For disposable,
   high-turnover jobs, it is the best choice available.
Q8：适合给小孩的玩具用吗？
A：非常适合，这也是评价里最典型的正面场景（玩具类提及 275 次）。
    便宜、量大、坏了不心疼，是"消耗型"场景的最优选择。
